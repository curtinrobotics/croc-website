**Curtin Robotics Club (CROC)** is teaming up with **UWA Robotics Club (URC)** to bring you the biggest, boldest Hackathon yet!

Design and build a robot to navigate obstacles and solve problems in an escape room like course. Work as a team to complete the challenge, score points and win judges favour.

Whether you're a seasoned builder or just starting out, get ready to dive into two full days of designing, coding, and competing in an electrifying robotics challenge.

#WHEN
Monday, September 1st, 10am Till Late

Tuesday, September 2nd, 9am - 6pm

#WHERE
Engineering Pavilion (Building 215, Room 100 – The Foyer), Curtin University

#WHO
**Open To:** any non-industry professional who wants to compete, we recommend teams of 3-5. If you come by yourself you will be assigned a team.

**Team Size:** 2–5 participants (we can match you up if you don’t have a team)

**Skill Level:**  Intermediate level challenge, if you have basic skills in Arduino, programming, electronics or mechanical design you should have what you need to compete, we provide basic documentation for all our systems to help. If you are a beginner it's recommended you come with a good team.

#WHAT TO EXPECT
**Day 1:** Brainstorm, plan & begin building

**Day 2:** Final build, play-test, and compete! As Judging takes place in the early evening

#WHAT TO BRING
Laptop & charger

Arduino IDE: Make sure it’s installed!
